The Artist

Makeup, made to suit the face in front of her.

Jiya Muradwani is a makeup artist based in Chhindwara. Her work is built around close attention — to skin, to bone structure, to the way a look holds up through a long day or an evening under lights.

Rather than repeating one signature look, she builds each face from its own starting point, drawing from bridal tradition, editorial reference and everyday glam alike.

She's drawn to the more artistic side of the craft — texture, colour and finish treated with the same care as a photograph — while keeping every look wearable for the person underneath it.
